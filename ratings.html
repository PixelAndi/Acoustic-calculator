Absorption ratings: NRC, SAA and αw

Type the sound absorption coefficients of a product. The tool calculates the single-number ratings of ASTM C423 and ISO 11654.

ISO 11654 steps

How to use

  1. Select the band type of your data: one-third octave or octave.
  2. Type the absorption coefficient for each band. Use the values from the test report.
  3. Read the NRC, SAA, αw, the shape indicators and the class.

Description

NRC (noise reduction coefficient) is the mean of the one-third-octave values at 250, 500, 1000 and 2000 Hz, rounded to the nearest 0.05.

SAA (sound absorption average) is the mean of the 12 one-third-octave values from 200 Hz to 2500 Hz, rounded to the nearest 0.01.

αp (practical sound absorption coefficient) is the mean of the three one-third-octave values in an octave. The mean is calculated to two decimals, then rounded in steps of 0.05. The maximum is 1.00.

αw (weighted sound absorption coefficient) comes from a reference curve. The curve moves down in steps of 0.05 until the sum of the unfavourable deviations is 0.10 or less. An unfavourable deviation is a value of αp below the curve. αw is the value of the curve at 500 Hz.

Shape indicators show where αp is 0.25 or more above the moved curve: L at 250 Hz, M at 500 Hz or 1000 Hz, H at 2000 Hz or 4000 Hz.

Absorption class: A (αw 0.90–1.00), B (0.80–0.85), C (0.60–0.75), D (0.30–0.55), E (0.15–0.25). Lower values are "not classified".

Method

ISO 11654 reference curve: 250 Hz 0.80 · 500 Hz 1.00 · 1000 Hz 1.00 · 2000 Hz 1.00 · 4000 Hz 0.90

Sources: SAA and NRC ; αp, αw, shape indicators and classes ; measurement in a reverberation room .

Limits

  • The standards use values measured in a reverberation room (ASTM C423 or ISO 354). Values from a prediction or from an impedance tube give only an estimate of the rating.
  • If you type octave values, the tool uses them as αp after rounding. The NRC then uses octave values, not one-third-octave values. This is an approximation. SAA needs one-third-octave values.
  • Negative values are set to 0.
