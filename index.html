About the calculations

The absorber pages use the transfer of impedance through layers. The models come from Cox & D'Antonio, Acoustic Absorbers and Diffusers. Automatic tests check the calculations against the "Porous Absorber Calculator V1.60" spreadsheet, against SciPy and against known physical limits.

The results are predictions. Real products can be different because of the size of the panel, the mounting and the quality of the material. For a final design, use measured data from a laboratory.

All calculations run in your browser. The tool does not send data to a server.
