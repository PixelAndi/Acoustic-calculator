References and methods

The calculators use published and established methods. This page lists all sources. Each calculator page also has a numbered reference list for its own methods.

How the sources were selected

  • Peer-reviewed papers (for example J. Acoust. Soc. Am., J. Audio Eng. Soc., Applied Acoustics), international standards (ISO, ITU, ASTM, DIN, EBU) and standard textbooks.
  • Each entry was checked against the publisher, the DOI system or the standards body. A link or DOI is given where one exists.
  • Some standards and books are not free to read. The link goes to the official page of the document.
  • Where a value comes from a secondary source (for example a manufacturer summary of a standard), the entry says so.
  • Papers of the Audio Engineering Society (AES) are included where they apply to a method (room modes, reflections, diffusers, membrane absorbers, scattering).
  • We also searched the public material of SAE Institute. Its public pages give general descriptions without authors, formulas or sources, so we do not cite them.
  • Where we could not confirm a formula in a source that we could read, the calculator page says so.
