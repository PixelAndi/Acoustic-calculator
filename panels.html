Absorbers and diffusers

An absorber removes sound energy. It decreases reverberation and noise. A diffuser does not remove much energy. It spreads the reflections in many directions, so the room stays "live" but has no strong echoes. Many rooms use both.

Porous absorbers work best at middle and high frequencies. Resonant absorbers work in a narrower band, often at low frequencies. A deep porous absorber, or a porous absorber with an air gap, can also absorb low frequencies.
